AI Behaviour Decision System with varying autonomy

Info

  • Engine: Godot
  • Languages: GDScript
  • Platform: PC
  • Time frame: 1 Month (20 Hours/Week)
  • Team Size: Solo

Overview

For my Bachelor's thesis I wanted to research video game AI companions. I wanted to find out whether players preferred companions they could exert more control over or companions that were fully autonomous.

To do this I created a design project where players would play the same exact simple game but with different AI types.

Contributions

  • ● Character controller that can be controlled by both player and AI input.
  • ● 3 different AI controller types with varying degrees of autonomy.
  • ● Encapsulated and reusable behaviours across AI types.
  • ● Utility based Behaviour Decision System that scores different behaviours.

Take aways

I learnt a lot about encapsulation on this project. I really tried to force myself to make my code reusable and it made the development process unsurprisingly a lot smoother.

I also deepened my understanding of video game AI not just from an implementation standpoint but also from a design one. From the results of my research I learnt about player behaviour and preference in regards to AI.
